About

is a remote job search tool. Instead of hosting its own listings, it gathers jobs live from established remote job boards and shows you where each one came from, so you can always check the original posting before you apply.

You choose where you are able to work (worldwide, a continent, or a specific country), choose the kind of work you want, and then narrow the list down by searching for a particular job.

How it works

  1. Pick your location. Choose Global, Continent or Country. For continents and countries, a list of every option in the world opens for you to choose from.
  2. Pick a job type. Choose a field such as software, design, support or marketing and see the matching jobs.
  3. Search for a particular job. Once jobs are listed, use the search bar to find a specific role, skill or company.
  4. Apply. Click Apply now on a job and you go straight to that job's application page. The job is also logged under My applications on your dashboard so you can track it.
